Reflections & Learning Log

My College Experience & Progress
Introduction to Artificial Intelligence October 2026

Progress, Not Perfection

Main Lesson Learned: As a first-year AI Engineering student at Mapúa University, I have learned that progress takes time and that I do not need to understand everything immediately. Building a strong foundation and developing my skills gradually are important steps toward reaching my goals. I also realized that learning is not just about getting good results but also about understanding concepts and improving through experience.

Challenges Encountered: Adjusting to fast-paced university schedules and grasping abstract foundational concepts was challenging, understanding unfamiliar technical concepts, especially in mathematics. Managing my time between different subjects and academic requirements can also be difficult. Since I am still adjusting to college, I need to learn how to handle these responsibilities while keeping up with my studies.

How I Can Improve: I can improve by managing my time wisely, practicing consistently, and reviewing lessons that I find difficult. I should also ask questions when I need help instead of allowing myself to fall behind. By setting realistic goals and recognizing the areas where I need improvement, I hope to develop my skills and become a more responsible and capable AI Engineering student.
